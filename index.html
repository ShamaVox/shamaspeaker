01 The Bio

Shama Keskar is a three-time founding CTO. But that is not where the story started.

She got into tech through the back door. Physics graduate. No CS degree. First job in the United States was cashier at a Big 5 sporting goods store in Silicon Valley. Broke into Google in 2004 as a QA engineer.

Then spent the next 15+ years scaling engineering across Google, LinkedIn, and Amazon — leading quality for Google's Online Advertising platform (scope: ~98% of company revenue), pioneering Alexa automation at Amazon, and launching Amazon Go and Amazon One as Amazon's first physical retail launches. Grew teams from 4 to 50+ engineers along the way.

In 2021 she went founding CTO. And has not stopped since — Nickelytics (edge AI + computer vision for autonomous outdoor advertising), SignAI (real-time bidirectional ASL/English AI interpreter prototype demoed to Microsoft), and most recently co-founder & founding CTO of an AI-native fashion wholesale platform where she built the production document extraction system that hit 96% accuracy across live paying merchants.

Currently writing her first book The Trust Layer — building AI systems you can actually ship. Her thesis is simple: generation is solved, trust is the product. She writes about it publicly on Substack and speaks on it wherever engineering leaders will let her.

The through-line from QA engineer at Google to three-time founding CTO has always been the same. Care about the boring engineering work everyone else skips. Evals, reliability, trust architecture, et al. Ship things that actually work in production.

02 Signature Talks

Four talks for four different rooms. Every one grounded in shipped work, not slideware.

01

Beyond DORA — The Eval Layer Every AI Team Needs

DORA covers deployment velocity beautifully but does not tell you whether the AI you are shipping actually works. This talk introduces the evals-and-confidence-scoring layer that has to sit alongside DORA in every AI-native team — how to build it, who owns it, and how to run it as a weekly signal instead of a launch gate.

Audience Engineering leaders
VPs of Engineering
Developer-productivity teams
CTOs
02

Generation Is Solved. Trust Is the Product.

Why the interesting question in 2026 is not "can the model do the thing" but "can the model do the thing every time." A framework for architecting production AI systems — the four-layer pattern shipped three times over, why the LLM should never be the calculator, and why the eval harness has to be built before the pipeline, not after.

Audience AI/ML engineers
Founding CTOs
Technical operators
Engineering leadership
03

Nobody Is Coming to Discover You

The engineering-not-luck approach to visibility, credibility, and building in public. Drawn from breaking into Google without a CS degree, launching three startups without a Bay Area pedigree, and turning underdog origin into an operator superpower.

The Decade No One Named
